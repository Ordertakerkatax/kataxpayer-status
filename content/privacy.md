We do not ask for your name, your TIN, your e-mail or your phone number, and a report cannot carry any of them.

To stop the same person reporting over and over, and to slow down spam, we keep two scrambled codes with each report:

- **A code for your browser.** Your browser gets a random number the first time you report. We store only a scrambled version of it, which cannot be turned back into the number.
- **A code for your network.** We scramble your internet address together with the day's date, so the code changes every day and cannot be matched from one day to the next. We never store the address itself.

Reports are kept to build the monthly and yearly availability record on the history page. Nobody outside KaTaxpayer sees a single report: the public board shows only counts, and the times on it are rounded down to the minute.

The check that you are a person is Cloudflare Turnstile. Cloudflare sees your internet address while it runs that check, under its own privacy policy, and tells us only whether you passed.

This site sets one cookie, the browser code above, and only when you send a report. There is no advertising or tracking on this site.
